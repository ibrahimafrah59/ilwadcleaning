Common questions

Do I need to move all my furniture?

No. We'll move any furniture one person can easily move, at no extra cost. Large or delicate pieces, like pianos, china cabinets, entertainment units, beds or antiques, need to be moved beforehand if you want us to clean underneath them.

Do you charge to move large furniture?

Yes. Moving large furniture can be added to your booking as an optional service.

A schedule that works for you

Weekly

What's included

Bathrooms

  • Clean and sanitize countertops, sink and toilet
  • Remove soap scum and light mildew from tubs and showers
  • Clean mirrors and polish fixtures
  • Dust light fixtures and countertop items
  • Empty trash and reline the can

Kitchen

  • Spot clean cabinet fronts and backsplash
  • Clean and sanitize countertops and sinks
  • Clean the stovetop, and the microwave inside and out
  • Polish appliance exteriors and sink fixtures
  • Empty trash and reline the can

All rooms

  • Make beds
  • Dust furniture, knick-knacks and wall art
  • Dust reachable ceiling fans and light fixtures
  • Dust baseboards, doors, blinds and window sills
  • Vacuum floors and mop hard floors

Add-ons (extra charge)

  • Inside the oven
  • Inside the fridge and freezer
  • Hand-washed baseboards
  • Hand-washed doors, frames and window sills

Common questions

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. Our team arrives with all the cleaning supplies and equipment needed for the job.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. It's fine to be out while we clean. We just ask that the space is ready for us when we arrive.

Are you insured?

Yes, Ilwad Cleaning is fully insured.

How do I pay?

We accept all payment methods: Square online, bank transfer, e-transfer, cash or card. We ask for a 50% deposit to book your cleaning.

Is the $30 off for new customers?

Yes. The $30 off is for first-time customers on their first home cleaning.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule 24 hours or more in advance and there's no charge. For less than 24 hours' notice we keep 25% of the deposit, and for less than 12 hours' notice we keep 50% of the deposit.

What areas do you cover?

We serve Toronto, including downtown, Regent Park, North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke and East York.
